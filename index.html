      Nappy guide

      Show the 24 hour guide Highlights wet and dirty nappy counts against a target.

      In the first week the usual guide is roughly one wet nappy per day of age, so Pip ramps the target up until day six. These are general figures, not advice about your baby. Check them against what your midwife or child and family health nurse tells you, and call them if anything worries you.
